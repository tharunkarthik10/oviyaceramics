The Manufacturing Process

A meticulous journey from raw earth to finished architectural element.

1

Material Preparation

We source the finest clays, felspars, and quartz. Through advanced milling processes, we achieve precise particle size distribution ensuring the structural integrity of every piece.

2

Forming

Utilizing high-pressure casting and precision extrusion, raw materials are shaped into their final forms with millimeter accuracy.

3

Firing

Computer-controlled kilns fire the ceramics at temperatures exceeding 1200°C, vitrifying the material for maximum durability.

4

Finishing & Quality Control

Each piece undergoes automated optical inspection and manual touch-ups, ensuring flawlessness before final packaging.
